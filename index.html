hi, i'm aarya.

i think the two greatest problems of our generation are building efficient, abundant, cheap energy through nuclear fusion, and speeding up scientific discovery with ai.

energy is the ceiling on what a civilization can do: what it can build, grow, compute and move. discovery is how fast we get there. raise the ceiling and speed up the climb, and almost everything else gets easier.

now

i'm at carnegie mellon, studying math and computer science.

before this

i took a gap year after high school to work as an ai engineer at palantir, through the meritocracy fellowship. i was on the hospitals team.

i saw two things up close. first, individuals can rewrite things into their own vision. second, the vision of the so-called giants is not always fully fleshed out. it often looks more finished from the outside than it is.

we are all just humans. if you have a good idea, you should go do it.
